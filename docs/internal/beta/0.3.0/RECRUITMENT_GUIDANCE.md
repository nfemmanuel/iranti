# Recruitment Guidance - Iranti Closed Beta v0.3.0

## Recommendation

Use a mixed cohort, but start with people you know who actually fit the target
workflow.

For a closed beta, response quality and completion rate matter more than broad
reach. People you know are more likely to:

- actually finish the test
- tolerate rough edges
- give detailed feedback
- tell you where the product is still weak

But if every tester knows you personally, feedback can become too forgiving.

## Suggested Mix

- 70-80% people you know and trust who fit the criteria
- 20-30% people you do not know personally but who clearly fit the use case

This gives you:

- higher completion rate
- higher-quality feedback
- some distance from founder bias

## Who Fits The Beta Best

Prioritize testers who:

1. already use AI heavily for coding
2. regularly stop and resume work across sessions
3. switch between hosts, tools, or agents
4. already feel the pain of rediscovery
5. are comfortable enough with terminal and repo workflows to notice whether continuity improved

Examples:

- heavy Claude Code / Codex users
- CLI-heavy developers
- technical founders doing real implementation work
- multi-host experimenters who start fresh sessions often

## Who Is Less Useful For First Wave

Avoid centering the first wave on:

- casual AI chat users
- people who stay in one long thread and rarely restart
- people who like the idea of memory but do not have a continuity problem

## Why A Single Focus Session Is Not Enough

Iranti is not mainly an onboarding product. Its value is in:

- persistence
- resumption
- cross-session continuity
- handoff

That means the beta should ask for usage over time, not just one focus session.

Recommended structure:

1. one guided setup session
2. 3-7 days of real use
3. one debrief or feedback form at the end

Minimum useful structure:

- one onboarding session
- two or more real work sessions on different days

## Beta Success Signal

Good testers should be able to answer:

- Did Iranti save me from re-explaining context?
- Did it help me resume work across sessions?
- Did it help when switching hosts or tools?
- Did I trust what it told me?
- Would I keep it on in real work?
